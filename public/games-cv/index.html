How I work

What the job actually is

Four things I do every week on a live mobile game.

01

UI vision

Set the look of a feature or a season with art direction and product, and hold it to a quality bar.

→
02

Typography & iconography

Type systems, icon sets, layout and hierarchy that survive a small screen and a dozen languages.

→
03

Design systems

Component libraries and written guidelines, so the team ships fast without the UI drifting.

→
04

Prototypes & hand-off

Interactive prototypes to settle arguments early, then specs and assets engineering can build from.

I use AI day to day for research, exploration and prototyping. It makes the exploring faster; it doesn't make the decisions.
